Receive invoices and attachments, extract fields, request missing information, and preserve the audit trail.

## Problem

Invoices arrive as messages and files, but most automation starts after a person manually moves them into another system.

## Workflow

1. Receive invoices directly in a dedicated mailbox.
2. Store attachment bytes outside the transactional database.
3. Extract vendor, amount, due date, and purchase-order references.
4. Draft exceptions for human approval before payment workflows continue.

AgentMailer supplies the communication substrate. Your runtime owns model execution, application state, schedules, and business approvals. Check the [examples catalog](https://github.com/aadi-labs/agentmailer-plugins/tree/main/examples) for Python and TypeScript implementations.
